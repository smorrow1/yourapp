## Summary

- We do **not** require an account.
- Your tanks, readings, doses, and settings are stored **only on your device**.
- We do **not** collect, sell, or transmit your aquarium data to our servers.

## Data stored on your device

The app stores the data you enter — tank details, water-test readings, dose and
water-change logs, and app settings — locally on your device using the
operating system's standard storage. This data never leaves your device except
when **you** explicitly export it (see below) or through a backup you have
configured with Apple or Google.

## Export / sharing

When you use **Export CSV**, the app creates a file and hands it to your
device's share sheet. What happens next (email, Files, another app) is entirely
your choice and governed by that destination's own privacy terms.

## Purchases

ReefPilot Pro is sold through the Apple App Store / Google Play. Payment is
processed by the store; we never receive or store your payment details. We use
**RevenueCat** to validate purchase receipts and manage entitlements. RevenueCat
receives a store-provided anonymous app user ID and purchase metadata — not your
name, email, or aquarium data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy for details.

## Notifications

If you enable test reminders, they are scheduled locally on your device. No data
is sent to us.

## Analytics & tracking

This version does **not** include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs and
does **not** track you across apps or websites.
